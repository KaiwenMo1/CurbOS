# Real-source readiness review template

Use this blank template for a privately retained assessment. Keep record-level findings, source archives, evidence locations, and credentials outside source control.

## 1. Executive decision

Record the technical, evidence, and publication outcomes independently. State the proposed Discovery claim and the exact decision that needs human review.

## 2. Source dataset identity

Record the scope, batch, publisher, dataset, stage digest, candidate ID, and report digest. Refer to the private component identity and acquisition records.

## 3. Technical pipeline results

Record Stage Schema, validator, projection and report versions. Confirm verification of the exact raw artifacts, database anchor, and candidate. Include registration status where available.

## 4. QA and quarantine

Record source-row denominators for Zones, Policies, and Areas. Separate accepted, review-required, projected, and excluded counts. Cite private references for unresolved QA, unusable Policies, directional relationship discrepancies, and withheld records.

## 5. Rights and redistribution

Record an independent decision for each checklist item. Cite the exact component, applicable terms and version, reviewed use, attribution, restrictions, and revocation requirements.

## 6. Geographic coverage

Record the reviewed boundary digest, projected-line denominator, containment and exclusions. State what the independent review establishes.

## 7. Curb placement

Describe the source geometry representation, alignment checks and street-side limitations. Distinguish independent placement evidence from source geometry and any category of unknowns.

## 8. Parking legality

Record unresolved legal backing, signs, restrictions, permits, and full-stay applicability. State the uncertainty in legality, occupancy, availability, and physical fit.

## 9. Publication security and withdrawal

Cite the relevant engineering controls and a separately reviewed operator and withdrawal plan. Distinguish disposable test results from real-data operating evidence.

## 10. Unresolved blockers

List each reason code, the exact required evidence, and the reviewer or authority responsible for supplying it.

## 11. Next engineering actions

Describe approved next review or verification work. Preserve private source evidence and existing publication controls.

## 12. Final recommendation

Record the assessment outcome and any separate authorization still required. A readiness record carries no activation authority.
