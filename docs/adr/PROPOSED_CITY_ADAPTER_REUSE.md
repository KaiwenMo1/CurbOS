# Proposed ADR — Reuse the regulation evaluator across cities

**Status** — Proposed for review, 2026-11-22. Baseline `75da062f4fdf0f3b6cad7df6aad6d2ab2a32562f` includes M3 PR-02.

## Decision for review

Keep one bounded evaluator. Add a city through a versioned source mapping, exact curb and jurisdiction bindings, and separately reviewed evidence. Reuse a format adapter across cities that provide compatible data. City-specific facts belong in a mapping profile and an evidence bundle. Leave unrepresentable effects, scope and applicability explicitly unresolved. The [accepted decisions](../architecture/DECISION_REGISTER.md), [ADR-028](ADR-028_VALIDATED_STAGING_ATOMIC_PUBLICATION.md), and the [M3 evaluation contract](../milestones/M3_RULE_EVALUATION_CONTRACT.md#coverage-and-negative-proof) remain authoritative.

## Architecture assessment

| Action | Module and reason |
| --- | --- |
| KEEP | The [UTC sweep](../../backend/internal/regulations/evaluate.go) and [proof checks](../../backend/internal/regulations/proof.go) own full-stay legality. Retain half-open boundaries, independent permission and coverage, restriction-specific negative proof, permit evidence and explicit unknowns. |
| KEEP | [Civil normalization](../../backend/internal/regulations/civil.go), [pinned timezone projection](../../backend/internal/regulations/civil_time.go) and [calendar handling](../../backend/internal/regulations/civil_calendar.go) supply absolute fragments and evidence. `CivilRule` and `SyntheticRule` share fields because they describe two stages of one rule. Their time representations and invariants differ. Keep the distinction between a calendar operation recorded during normalization and an exclusion cited by a permitted result. |
| REUSE | The [private CDS candidate pipeline](../../data/cds_validate/README.md) already checks source lineage and retains quarantine. Use verified private artifact references and dispositions at the adapter seam. Structural checks during capture and independent rechecks at candidate validation serve distinct trust boundaries. |
| SIMPLIFY | On the successful civil path, `NormalizeCivil` validates the absolute snapshot and sorts events to check boundary limits. `EvaluateCivil` calls `Evaluate`, which repeats both. Consider a private prepared-input path with parity tests and the same failure precedence. The rule and coverage projection loops also repeat work structurally, but their unresolved evidence differs. Leave them until a shared projection operation clearly reduces branching. |
| DEFER | A city plugin registry, canonical SQL schema changes, third-party runtime recurrence, a generic priority hierarchy, and public eligibility. M3 PR-03 owns reviewed authority and applicability under separate authorization. |

Measured against the baseline, the regulation package contains 2,037 non-test Go lines. The spike removes zero runtime lines and adds zero runtime dependencies. Estimate 15–40 net lines could be removed or avoided by sharing preparation and simple projection code. The main expected gain is one fewer validation and event sort on a successful run. No speedup was measured. There is no measured per-city implementation or credible line-count estimate for it yet.

## Candidate adapter interface

Reuse the M3 contract’s existing `NormalizedRule`, `EvidenceRecord` and `CoverageReview` designs. The minimum candidate rule carries a logical rule ID/version, exact resource/geometry revision/orientation/jurisdiction/side, one typed effect with applicable qualifiers, effective bounds and exactly one absolute or civil schedule. Preserve permit class/scope/issuer and a positive elapsed cap where supported. Evidence references resolve to dataset, batch, artifact SHA-256 and record locator, plus the adapter/profile version. Source as-of, observation time and legal effective time are distinct. Treat certainty about scope separately from certainty about the effect.

```text
Adapt(verifiedPrivateArtifact, versionedCityMappingProfile)
    -> candidateRules, unresolvedFacts, error
```

This is a proposed private function signature. The mapping profile specifies source format, vocabulary mappings and reviewed target/jurisdiction binding inputs. Candidate effects and reason-coded unresolved facts cite raw records, with the broadest possible scope where bounds are uncertain. Integrity mismatches fail the mapping. Recoverable unsupported constructs retain the gap. A separate approved review supplies legal coverage, authority, temporary-order disposition and any evidence needed for permit or vehicle facts. Pin timezone and calendar data with that review. Never pass decoder output off as fixture-only proof premises. The existing ADR-028 capture and publication boundaries continue to apply.

## Two-city demonstration

From `backend`, run `go test -count=1 -v ./internal/regulations/... -run '^TestSyntheticCityAdapterSpike$'`.

| Synthetic configuration | Observed outcome for `[05:15Z,06:35Z)` on 2026-11-01 |
| --- | --- |
| East, left side, pinned fold, Sunday 01:20–01:30 | Prohibited over `[05:20Z,05:30Z)` and `[06:20Z,06:30Z)`. The intervening gap is permitted under the explicit fixture premises. |
| West, right side, fixed −08:00, Saturday 21:30–22:00 | Prohibited over `[05:30Z,06:00Z)`. Remaining portions are permitted under the explicit fixture premises. |
| West with required calendar absent | `COMPLETE / UNKNOWN / CALENDAR_UNRESOLVED` across the stay, retaining the source reference. |
| West with East’s timezone binding | `FAILED / UNKNOWN / SNAPSHOT_INVALID`, with zero intervals. |

Both city profiles use the same test mapping and existing `EvaluateCivil`. The demonstration exercises supplied synthetic coverage and restriction premises. It leaves raw format parsing, real-source authority, review and performance unmeasured. The third-party recurrence probe ran in a separate temporary Go module using `go run -mod=mod .` with `FREQ=WEEKLY;COUNT=3`, Go’s `America/New_York` location, and starts `2026-03-01 02:10` and `2026-10-25 01:20`. It tested recurrence points only. Other upstream software was inspected without running it.

## Next three small PRs for separate approval

1. Characterize and reuse civil preparation privately. Check exact intervals and fail-closed precedence at limits. Keep `NormalizeCivil` independently usable. Measure repeat validation and sort costs before claiming a speed improvement.
2. Add one synthetic format-conformance fixture distinct from CDS, such as a CurbLR sample with a compound time clause and ambiguous side. Preserve raw IDs and unsupported gaps. Extract a shared mapper only if the two formats expose a common contract.
3. After independent rights and evidence review, map one eligible retained private source through one narrow format adapter. Compare provenance, geometry revision, unknowns and deterministic replay against the synthetic corpus. Keep the output private and leave M3 PR-03 authority and publication gates under their existing approvals.

**Risks.** Feed priority is a source claim. A change to geometry or direction can invalidate an earlier curb-side review. A missing sign, temporary order or relevant exception can invalidate positive proof even when all supplied rows parse. The narrow schedule subset may leave many genuine rules unresolved. Revert this spike commit to remove the proposed ADR and the test.

**Engineering Learning Note.** The same UTC request crosses a DST fold in one fixture and the previous local date in the other. Keeping time interpretation in a single bounded normalizer lets each city supply its own premises without implementing its own legality logic.
